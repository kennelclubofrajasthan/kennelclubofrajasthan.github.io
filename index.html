Dog Shows & Pet Events

Celebrating Dogs. Advancing Responsible Care.

The Kennel Club of Rajasthan (KCR) organises dog shows and pet events to encourage informed canine ownership, scientific knowledge of breeding, breed standards and a healthy, welcoming environment for dog enthusiasts.

The club promotes dog welfare, ethical breeding practices and public education through shows, seminars, awareness programmes and community gatherings.

According to KCR's historical introduction, its organised dog-show journey began in 2003. Its continuing championship tradition has helped make canine activities part of Rajasthan's cultural and family calendar.

The People Behind Our Legacy

Viren Sharma
A Legacy of Love & Leadership

Founding Secretary, Kennel Club of Rajasthan
Visionary Entrepreneur · Canine Culture Pioneer · Animal Welfare Advocate

For Viren Sharma, dogs have never been merely pets. They are family, lifelong companions and a source of unconditional love. For 27 years, he has helped shape Rajasthan’s canine culture through championship dog shows, public education, responsible pet parenting and compassionate animal welfare.

As KCR’s Founding Secretary, a leading force behind International Dog Bazar (IDB) and founder of PAWS — Protection of Animals & Welfare Society, he has worked to bring dog lovers, families, breeders and animal welfare advocates together.

Discover Viren Sharma’s journey, initiatives & recognitions

27 Years of Purpose

Viren Sharma’s personal passion for animals became a sustained mission to help Rajasthan understand, welcome, celebrate and care for its four-legged companions. His work has connected entrepreneurship, canine education, organised events and compassion for animals in need.

International Dog Bazar — A Family Legacy

Building on a family enterprise with roots dating to 1957, Viren helped develop International Dog Bazar into a broad pet-care destination. Its services encompass pet selection counselling, veterinary care, nutrition, vaccination, grooming, training, boarding, hospitality, accessories and ongoing guidance. IDB reports a customer community exceeding 100,000.

Building Rajasthan’s Canine Culture

As the Founding Secretary of the Kennel Club of Rajasthan, Viren has supported championship shows, breed awareness, dog talk shows, seminars and responsible ownership. According to the club’s supplied history, championship shows have been organised since 2003, reaching 28 editions and welcoming a cumulative audience of more than 1.1 million visitors.

He has also promoted dog carnivals, canine sports, fashion walks, adoption drives and family-friendly pet festivals, making canine culture accessible beyond traditional specialist circles.

PAWS — Compassion in Action

As founder of PAWS (Protection of Animals & Welfare Society), Viren advocates dignity and care for homeless, abandoned and injured animals. Initiatives include:

  • Street-animal feeding, including during COVID-19 lockdowns
  • Adoption, rescue and rehabilitation
  • Mission PAANI — drinking water for street animals in summer
  • Shining Paws — reflective collars for road safety
  • Thand Sabko Lagti Hai — winter warmth and protection
  • Pet education and community awareness

Awards & Recognition

His supplied professional profile lists honours including World Record of India Recognition, World Book of Records Recognition, Rajasthan Gaurav Award, Rajasthan Radiance Award, Rajasthan Achievers Award, Jaipur Ratna Award, Samaj Ratna Award, National Brahmin Ratna Award, National Pet Icon Award, IIPTF National Award, My FM Jiyo Dil Se Award, Most Noble Person of the Year (Elite Mrs. Rajasthan) and the Indian Pet Industry Award.

His experience has also led to invitations to judge dog shows in Jodhpur, Udaipur, Bikaner, Aligarh, Kota and other cities. Award titles and recognitions are presented as supplied in his biography, pending supporting documentation.

A Vision Beyond Business

Viren envisions Rajasthan as a destination for canine excellence, pet education, responsible ownership and animal welfare. His ambition is not simply to create more pet owners, but to inspire better pet parents and a more compassionate society — one in which every animal, pedigree or homeless, is treated with dignity.
